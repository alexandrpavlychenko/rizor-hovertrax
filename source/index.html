Design

The hoverboard's design embodies the spirit of futurism:

Color

Indicators

LED indicators let others know when you’re about to turn. They respond instantly to shifts in your centre of gravity to the left or right by flashing.

Once you’re moving straight ahead again, the flashing stops and the lights return to a solid line.

Modes

In addition to its ‘smart’ lighting, the Hovertrax 4.0 features two speed modes.

  • One for advanced users

  • The other for beginners

Characteristics

Rizor Hovertrax 4.0

  • Housing

    it is made of durable plastic and features attractive LED lighting that complements the overall design. The foot platforms have a slippery surface.

  • Wheels

    The Rizor Hovertrax 4.0 features relatively small, but generally medium‑diameter, 6.5‑inch wheels.

  • Power

    the 350 W motor provides a comfortable and safe ride at a maximum speed of 13 km/h.

  • Weight

    its low weight of approximately 12 kg makes the hoverboard relatively compact and convenient to carry around.

Who It’s For

With a maximum load capacity of 100 kg, it is ideal for both the younger generation and more mature ‘riders’.

It is the only hoverboard with a special riding mode for children. It ensures a smooth ride and helps prevent most riding mistakes.
